# Character Bible: Erika Mercer

Everything here is fixed. If a generated frame breaks one of these, regenerate it.

## Identity

- **Name:** Erika Mercer
- **Tagline:** The other Mercer. iykyk.
- **Vibe:** 2000s alt girl who trains like a fighter. Quiet, a little smug, never explains herself.

## Face (never changes)

- Dark brown eyes, heavy smoky liner, slightly hooded look
- Full lips, muted pink-nude, soft gloss
- Straight nose, defined cheekbones
- Olive-tan skin, light realistic texture, no airbrushing
- Expression default: neutral, half-bored, direct eye contact

## Hair (never changes)

- Jet black, shaggy layered wolf cut / mullet
- Long choppy curtain bangs that partly cover the eyes
- Ends past the shoulders, piecey and textured, slightly messy

## Body

- Athletic and muscular: defined arms, visible abs, strong shoulders
- Reads as someone who actually trains, not a fitness model pose

## Signature outfit (default in every clip unless a scene says otherwise)

- Heather gray cropped baby tee, distressed with small rips at the chest
- Pink claw-mark graphic on the front, outlined in black
- Black baggy cargo pants, faded wash, lots of pockets with buckles and D-rings
- Black studded belt with a hanging silver chain on the left hip
- Black studded leather wrist cuffs on both wrists
- Chunky black platform combat boots

## Personality

- Says little. Lets the action talk.
- Dry humour, no explaining the joke.
- Unbothered in absurd situations (sparring a monk, flipping off a roof).
- Captions in lowercase, short, never more than one line.

## Content format

- One situation per clip, 5 to 12 seconds
- Hook in the first second: she is already mid-action
- Settings rotate hard: temples, mountains, rooftops, gyms, bedrooms, streets
- The contrast is the joke: alt girl outfit in a place it shouldn't be

## Consistency checklist (run on every frame)

- [ ] Bangs cover part of the eyes
- [ ] Hair is black, shaggy, shoulder-length or longer
- [ ] Gray crop tee with pink claw graphic
- [ ] Studded cuffs on both wrists
- [ ] Belt chain visible when hips are in frame
- [ ] Face matches the reference sheet at a glance
