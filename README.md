# erika-mercer

The full build process behind Erika Mercer, an AI character who made $10K from ads in her first 5 days.

This repo is the open playbook: how she looks, how she thinks, and the exact prompt structure used to keep her consistent across every video.

## Structure

```
erika-mercer/
├── CHARACTER.md        who she is: look, body, outfit, voice, rules
├── PIPELINE.md         the 3-tool workflow from idea to posted clip
└── prompts/
    ├── 01-image-chatgpt.md     character sheet + scene stills
    ├── 02-scenes-claude.md     personality and scene generation
    └── 03-video-higgsfield.md  motion prompts
```

## The one rule

**Lock the face, change the world.**

Every clip opens on something new (a temple, a cliff, a rooftop) but the face, hair and outfit never drift. Viewers recognise her in the first frame and stay to see what she does next.
