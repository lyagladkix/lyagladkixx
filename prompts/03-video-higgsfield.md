# Higgsfield: motion prompts

Upload the approved still from ChatGPT, then:

```
[ACTION in one sentence, present tense]. Natural human motion, realistic weight and momentum. Hair moves with the movement. Face stays consistent and recognisable throughout. Handheld phone camera feel, [CAMERA MOVE]. No morphing, no extra limbs, no outfit changes.
```

## Tips

- Shorter clips hold the face better. Stitch two short clips instead of one long one.
- Name one action only. Two actions in one prompt is where limbs break.
- If the face drifts, regenerate from the still; don't try to fix it in the next clip.
- Fast action (strikes, flips, jumps) hides small artifacts and gets more views anyway.
